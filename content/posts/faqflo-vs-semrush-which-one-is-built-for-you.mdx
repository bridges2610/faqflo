## Why I built FaqFlo

I’ve been a marketer for more than twenty years. I pay for Semrush. I still open it and lose ten
minutes finding the number I came in for.

That’s not a complaint about Semrush. It’s a very powerful tool, and powerful tools have a lot of
surface. But if two decades in this job doesn’t make it feel obvious, I don’t know which business
owner is supposed to feel differently.

So that’s the reason FaqFlo exists. Not because there’s a gap in the data, but because there’s a gap
in who the data is written for. You should be able to look at one screen and understand how AI sees
your business — without a course, a glossary, or a marketer sitting next to you.

That’s the whole product. Everything below is just the detail.

## Let’s be fair to Semrush first

Semrush is not a bad product. It’s one of the best-known marketing platforms in the world, it has
been around for years, and the data behind it is genuinely deep. Adobe completed its purchase of the
company in April 2026 for about $1.9 billion, which tells you roughly how seriously the industry
takes it.

If your job title has the word “marketing” in it, Semrush is probably already open in one of your
tabs. Nothing in this article is an argument that it’s overpriced or overrated.

It’s an argument that it was built for someone who isn’t you.

## What Semrush actually is

Semrush is a suite. Dozens of tools in one login: keyword research, backlink checking, rank tracking,
ads, social posting, content planning, competitor research. The main plans run $139.95, $249.95 and
$499.95 a month depending on how much you need.

More recently they added AI answers to the mix. The AI Visibility Toolkit costs $99/month per domain
and tracks 25 prompts across ChatGPT, Google’s AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini and Perplexity.
There’s also a bundle called Semrush One that folds the SEO tools and the AI tracking together,
starting at $199/month.

There’s a free account with limited features, and a free AI visibility checker you can run without
paying. Those are real, and they’re useful.

But look at the shape of the thing. It’s a platform. Platforms are priced on the assumption that
you’ll use a lot of them, most days, because using them is your job.

## What FaqFlo is

FaqFlo does one thing, and it’s narrow on purpose.

You put in your web address. We fetch your home page the way an AI crawler does and tell you, in
plain English, whether it can read you at all — and who got named instead of you when we put three
real questions to ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. That part is free, and it doesn’t ask for a card.

Then, if you want the rest: we find the questions people actually put to AI about your category and
your area, write answers built to be quoted, and hand you a block of plain HTML to paste onto your
own website. After that we watch 25 questions for you every week and show you whether your name
started coming up.

That’s the whole product. $39/month, or free if you only want to know where you stand.

## The difference that actually matters

Here’s the split, and it’s bigger than price.

Semrush is a measuring tool. It’s very good at telling you what’s true. Your visibility score is
this. Your competitor is named for these questions and you aren’t. This page has these problems.
That’s real information, and for a marketer it’s exactly the right handoff — because a marketer
knows what to do next.

FaqFlo is a fixing tool. The measurement is the first ten seconds, not the product. The product is
the block of HTML you paste into your site so the crawler has something to read.

That gap sounds small until you’re standing in it. A report that says “your content isn’t readable by
AI crawlers” is a finding. It is not a fix. Somebody still has to write the answers, format them
correctly, put them in the right place on the site, and check that they actually landed as readable
text. If you have a person for that, the report is the whole value. If you don’t, the report is
homework.

## The vocabulary problem

Open Semrush and you’ll meet keyword difficulty, domain authority, share of voice, crawl budget,
prompt volume, SERP features. Every one of those is a real concept that a marketer uses daily.

Every one of them is also a thing you’d have to go learn.

We took a hard line on this with FaqFlo. No credits. No seats. No API in the menu. One box, one
button, and an answer you can read without a glossary. When a feature required teaching you a new
word, we usually cut the feature.

That’s not us being clever. It’s the only version of this that a roofer will actually finish.

## Who should pick which

We’ll be straight about this, because pretending otherwise wastes your money and our time.

**Pick Semrush if you’re a marketer working for a big company.** If this is your actual job — if you
have the time to learn a platform properly and the volume to justify it — you’ll get far more out of
a full suite than out of us. Same goes if you need the other work done: paid ads, backlinks, ranking
for hundreds of keywords, managing a stack of client sites. We don’t do any of that and aren’t going
to.

**Pick FaqFlo if you’re the owner, the marketing department, and the person who answers the phone.**
If what you want is a clear answer to “can AI see my business, and if not, what do I paste where” —
that’s the entire thing we built.

Or do both. Plenty of businesses will. There’s nothing stopping you from running a full platform and
still using something small that hands you finished content. They don’t conflict.

And one more thing worth saying plainly: none of this replaces regular SEO. A healthy site that ranks
well is still the foundation, and it always will be. [Getting quoted by AI](/blog/what-is-aeo) is the
next floor up, not a replacement for the one underneath.

## Where FaqFlo comes in

If you’ve read this far and thought *I don’t have a marketing person, I just want to know if I’m
invisible* — that’s the exact sentence we built FaqFlo around.

[Put your web address in](/free-report). In about ten seconds you’ll know whether ChatGPT, Perplexity
and Gemini can read your site, what your AI-visibility score is, and which competitor got named when
we asked a question you should have won. No card, no call, no new vocabulary.

If you like what you see, Pro writes the answers and hands you the code to paste. If you don’t, you
keep the free report and you’ve lost ten seconds.

That’s the test I set for myself when I started building it: could I hand this to a plumber and have
him understand it without me in the room. If we ever fail that test, tell us.
