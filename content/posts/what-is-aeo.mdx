Your next customer might never see your website. Here’s how to get quoted anyway.

Someone opens ChatGPT and types, “What’s the best bakery near me for a custom birthday cake?” A few
seconds later they have their answer — three names, a sentence about each, maybe a link. They pick
one and place an order.

Notice what didn’t happen: they never Googled. They never scrolled a list of ten blue links. They
never landed on anyone’s homepage. The AI just… answered. And whoever it named won the customer.

That shift has a name, and it’s the whole ballgame for getting found in 2026. It’s called
[AEO](/#aeo) — Answer Engine Optimization — and if SEO was about ranking, AEO is about being the
answer.

## So what is AEO, exactly?

Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring your content so AI answer engines —
ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google’s AI Overviews, and the rest — can understand it, trust it, and quote
you directly when someone asks a question you can answer.

The goal isn’t a click. It’s a citation. When the AI writes its answer, your business is the source
it pulls from. You become the shop it recommends, the expert it quotes, the name it drops.

Think of it as the difference between showing up in the phone book and being the friend someone
asks for a recommendation. SEO got you into the book. AEO makes you the friend.

## Why this matters right now

Search behavior is changing fast, and it’s not subtle.

Analysts expect traditional search volume to drop meaningfully over the next couple of years as
people lean on AI assistants instead. Hundreds of millions of people already use AI tools every
week to research purchases, compare options, and get answers they used to hunt for across a dozen
tabs.

Here’s the part that trips people up: these are zero-click answers. The person gets what they need
inside the AI and often never visits a website at all. That sounds like bad news — until you
realize the businesses being named in those answers are winning the customer without the customer
ever clicking. One well-known finance site watched its traffic fall while its revenue climbed,
precisely because it kept showing up as the trusted source AI pointed people toward.

So the question stops being “How do I rank #1?” and becomes “When an AI answers a question about my
business, am I the answer — or is my competitor?”

Right now, for most small businesses, the honest answer is: neither, because the AI can’t even see
them.

## AEO vs. SEO: what actually changes

AEO doesn’t replace SEO — a strong site still helps, and the two work together. But the playbook is
different in a few important ways.

SEO optimizes whole pages to earn clicks; AEO optimizes bite-sized, quotable answers to earn
citations. SEO leans on backlinks and domain authority; AEO leans on clarity, structure, and being
easy to extract. SEO measures traffic and rankings; AEO measures how often you get mentioned and
quoted. And where SEO rewards long, comprehensive pages, AEO rewards a direct answer delivered in
the first sentence — because that’s the chunk an AI can lift cleanly into its response.

In short: SEO wants your page to win a list. AEO wants your answer to be the answer.

## How to actually do AEO

The good news is that AEO isn’t mysterious. It comes down to a handful of habits.

**Lead with the answer.** Put a clear, 30–60 word response right at the top of every topic, before
the backstory and the buildup. Answer engines grab the cleanest, most direct answer they can find.
Make yours the easiest to grab.

**Write in real questions and answers.** Structure content the way people actually ask — “How much
does X cost?” “Do you offer Y?” — with a straight answer under each. This is why FAQ content is
such a natural fit for AEO: it’s already shaped exactly like the questions people type into AI.
(More on that in a second.)

**Keep it factual and specific.** Names, places, prices, hours, specifics. Answer engines favor
content that’s concrete and unambiguous over fluffy marketing copy. “We’re passionate about
quality” gets ignored. “We hand-decorate custom cakes with 48 hours’ notice in Asheville” gets
quoted.

**Make it crawlable.** This is the one almost everyone gets wrong. Most FAQ and content widgets
render their answers with JavaScript — and AI crawlers generally don’t run JavaScript. That means
the answer looks perfect to a human visitor and is completely invisible to the AI. Your content has
to exist as real, crawlable HTML on your own website, or the engines that matter simply never see
it.

**Add structure and let engines check their work.** Clean HTML plus supporting
[schema](/seo-guide) (and a tidy llms.txt) gives answer engines explicit signals about what your
content means, so they can extract it confidently.

**Then track it.** AEO isn’t set-and-forget. New questions come up, competitors move, answers
change. The businesses that win keep an eye on whether they’re actually getting cited across
ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google’s AI answers — and fix the gaps when they appear.
