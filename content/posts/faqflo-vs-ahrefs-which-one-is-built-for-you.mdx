## Why I built FaqFlo

I’ve been in marketing for more than twenty years. I’ve used the big platforms. They’re excellent,
and I still lose ten minutes in one looking for a single number I came in for.

That’s not a knock on the tools. Powerful software has a lot of surface, and that surface is the
point when it’s your full-time job. But if two decades in the industry doesn’t make it feel obvious,
I don’t know which business owner is supposed to feel differently.

So that’s why FaqFlo exists. Not because there’s a gap in the data — there isn’t, the data out there
is very good. The gap is in who the data is written for. You should be able to look at one screen and
understand how AI sees your business, without a course, a glossary, or a marketer sitting next to
you.

That’s the whole product. Everything below is detail.

## Let’s be fair to Ahrefs first

Ahrefs is not a lightweight tool with a marketing budget. It’s one of the most respected SEO
platforms in the world, and the reason is unglamorous: they run their own web crawler and their own
index. When you look up who links to a competitor, that’s real data they collected, not a guess.

If you do SEO professionally, Ahrefs is probably already open in a tab, and nothing in this article
argues it shouldn’t be. This isn’t a piece about Ahrefs being overpriced or overrated.

It’s a piece about it being built for someone who probably isn’t you.

## What Ahrefs actually is

Ahrefs is a suite of research tools in one login: backlink analysis, keyword research, rank tracking,
site auditing, content research, competitor analysis.

Pricing is a ladder. There’s a small Starter plan at around $29 a month, then the three tiers most
people actually buy — Lite at $129, Standard at $249, Advanced at $449 — and an Enterprise tier
starting around $1,499. Paying yearly takes roughly two months off. Each plan includes one user;
extra people on your team cost extra per seat.

**AI visibility is separate.** Their AI tracking product, Brand Radar, is an add-on rather than
something bundled into the plan you’re already paying for. Public pricing puts it at about $199 a
month per AI platform, or around $699 a month for all of them, with custom prompt-tracking packages
starting around $50 a month on top. It also requires an active base subscription — so full AI
coverage lands near $828 a month once you add the cheapest qualifying plan underneath it.

So if what brought you here is “I want to know whether ChatGPT mentions my business,” the realistic
Ahrefs answer is a base plan plus an add-on. There’s also a free tier, Ahrefs Webmaster Tools, which
gives verified site owners a limited look at their own site — real and genuinely useful, but it isn’t
the AI part.

Look at the shape of the thing. It’s a research platform. Platforms are priced on the assumption that
you’ll open a lot of them, most days, because opening them is your job.

## What FaqFlo is

FaqFlo does one thing, narrow on purpose.

You put in your web address. We fetch your page the way an AI crawler does and tell you, in plain
English, whether it can read you at all — plus who got named instead of you when we put real customer
questions to the AI engines. That part is free and doesn’t ask for a card.

Then, if you want the rest: we find the questions people actually ask AI about your category and your
area, write answers built to be quoted, and hand you a block of plain HTML to paste onto your own
website. After that we watch 25 questions for you every week and show you whether your name started
coming up.

That’s it. $39 a month, or free if you only want to know where you stand.

## The difference that actually matters

Here’s the split, and it’s bigger than price.

**Ahrefs is a research tool.** It is very good at telling you what’s true. These sites link to your
competitor. This page has these errors. You’re mentioned in this share of AI answers and they’re
mentioned in that share. For a marketer, that’s exactly the right handoff — because a marketer knows
what to do next.

**FaqFlo is a fixing tool.** The measurement is quick and isn’t the product. The product is the block
of HTML you paste into your site so the crawlers have something to read.

That gap sounds small until you’re standing in it. A report that says “your content isn’t readable by
AI crawlers” is a finding. It is not a fix. Somebody still has to write the answers, format them
correctly, get them onto the right page, and check that they landed as readable text instead of
[JavaScript the crawler skips](/blog/why-faq-plugins-arent-a-good-idea). If you have a person for
that, the report is the whole value. If you don’t, the report is homework.

## The vocabulary problem

Open a platform like Ahrefs and you’ll meet domain rating, referring domains, keyword difficulty,
crawl credits, share of voice, prompt volume. Every one of those is a real concept a professional
uses daily.

Every one of them is also a thing you’d have to go learn.

We took a hard line on this. No credits. No seats. No API in the menu. One box, one button, and an
answer you can read without a glossary. When a feature required teaching you a new word, we usually
cut the feature.

That’s not us being clever. It’s the only version of this a roofer will actually finish.

## Who should pick which

We’ll be straight about this, because pretending otherwise wastes your money and our time.

**Pick Ahrefs if SEO is somebody’s actual job.** If you’re doing link building, competitive research,
keyword strategy across hundreds of terms, or managing a stack of client sites, you’ll get far more
out of a full research platform than out of us. We don’t do any of that and we’re not going to. Their
crawler and their index are the real product, and nothing small replaces them.

**Pick FaqFlo if you’re the owner, the marketing department, and the person who answers the phone.**
If what you want is a clear answer to “can AI see my business, and if not, what do I paste where” —
that’s the entire thing we built.

**Or do both.** Plenty of businesses will. Everything FaqFlo produces is standard HTML and standard
schema, so it drops into whatever else you’re running without conflict.

And one more thing worth saying plainly: none of this replaces regular SEO. A healthy site that ranks
well is still the foundation, and it always will be. Getting quoted by AI is the next floor up, not a
replacement for the one underneath.

## Where FaqFlo comes in

If you’ve read this far and thought *I don’t have an SEO person, I just want to know if I’m
invisible* — that’s the exact sentence we built FaqFlo around.

Put your web address in. In about ten seconds you’ll know whether the AI engines can read your site
and what your AI-visibility score is. The answers themselves — which competitor got named when we put

No card, no call, no new vocabulary.

If you like what you see, Pro writes the answers and hands you the code to paste. If you don’t, you
